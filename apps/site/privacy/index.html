Privacy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Overview

Polymux is a personal app. It connects to a Google account only so that the account owner's own agents can read that owner's data when asked. This page explains what Google data Polymux accesses and how it is handled. The general Polymux Privacy Policy covers the rest of the product.

Google data Polymux accesses

With your permission, Polymux requests access to:

  • Google Drive
  • Google Docs
  • Google Sheets
  • Gmail (read-only)
  • Google Calendar

This access is used only to let your own agents read your data to complete tasks you request. It is not used for anything else.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your own machine. They are not uploaded to a Polymux server.

Sharing and sale of data

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Polymux's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke Polymux's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Select Polymux and remove its access. You can also delete the locally stored tokens from your machine.
